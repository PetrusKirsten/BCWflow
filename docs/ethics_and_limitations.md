# Ethics and limitations

This project uses public/proxy data and must avoid overstating conclusions.

## Limitations

- Queue-time records are not official visitor counts.
- Closed rides may appear with zero wait time and must be handled carefully.
- Weather may influence behavior, but simple correlation is not causation.
- Public crowd calendars may include predictions or aggregated estimates.

## Ethical collection rules

- Prefer public APIs over scraping.
- Respect terms of use, robots.txt and rate limits.
- Do not publish personal data.
- Do not publish review text in a way that exposes individuals.
- Include clear disclaimers that the project is independent.
